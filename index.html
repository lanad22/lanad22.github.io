About

I'm a researcher at Northeastern's Khoury College of Computer Sciences, working on the YouDescribe project under Professor Ilmi Yoon, in collaboration with the Smith-Kettlewell Eye Research Institute. My work focuses on AI-assisted audio description for blind and low-vision users, spanning system development, user studies, and evaluation methodology.

I'm also the founder of Mosaic Cognition, where I build and research MOSAIC, an authoring tool that turns stories into structured comics to support reading comprehension and story composition for autistic learners.

In Fall 2026, I am honored to be an instructor for CS 5002: Discrete Structures at Northeastern's Silicon Valley campus.

I'm broadly interested in human-centered AI, accessibility, and how people and AI can collaborate on creative tasks.
